# Shea & Cocoa Butter Bar — Unscented

### Two Brambleberry 6-Cavity Ovals — Cold Process
### Batch #7 — Draft

> **Mold:** two Brambleberry 6-Cavity Oval molds, poured as one batch (3.5" × 2.5" × 1" per cavity)
> **Yield:** 12 bars | **Cure:** 6–8 weeks (4 minimum) | **Active:** ~1 hour
> **Basis:** a new line — the first batch with shea and cocoa butter, the first with no fragrance, and the first poured across both oval molds. A bath bar that also works at the sink
> **Safety:** glasses and gloves on before touching lye · lye into water, never the reverse · ventilate · soap-only equipment

## Recipe

| Component | Amount | Notes |

| Fragrance | none | Unscented by design |

> **7% superfat** — a point above the hand bar, for the mildest bar in the archive; the hard butters offset the softness a higher superfat usually costs. Blend **45% olive / 20% coconut / 15% shea / 10% cocoa butter / 10% castor**: olive drops below the 62% house floor to make room for 25% butters, coconut stays inside its 18–28% rail, and castor stays pinned at 10%. No fragrance, so nothing browns and nothing accelerates. Fills both ovals to 94.0% — 12 bars.

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Gear Up
Glasses and gloves on. Ventilation on. Pre-weigh every ingredient into its own container before anything is combined. There is no fragrance to stage.

### Step 2 — Prep the Molds
Set both oval molds side by side on a flat surface. No prep.

### Step 3 — Lye Solution *(make it first — it needs ~30 min to cool)*
1. Weigh **407g distilled water** into the 2qt stainless bowl
2. Weigh **143g lye** separately
3. **Pour lye into water** — never the reverse. Stir until clear; it heats to ~185°F and gives off brief fumes. Don't lean over it
4. Set it aside to cool to **110–120°F**
5. Stir **12ml sodium lactate** into the cooled solution

### Step 4 — Butters, Oils, Clay
1. Melt **107g cocoa butter**, **161g shea butter** and **214g coconut oil** together in the 6qt stockpot on the induction element, on low, until completely clear — no specks left
2. Take the pot off the heat and add **482g olive** and **107g castor**
3. **Stick-blend 21g kaolin clay straight into the combined oils** until smooth — no slurry cup
4. Bring the oils to **110–120°F**, within ~10°F of the lye solution. Stay in the upper half of the usual window: cocoa butter starts to set below ~95°F, and a cooling pot thickens into a false trace

### Step 5 — Combine and Trace
1. Pour the cooled lye solution into the stockpot — blend in place, no transfer
2. Stick-blend in **5–10 second bursts**, alternating with hand stirring, until **light trace** (the batter thickens just enough to leave a faint trail off the blender)
3. The butters bring trace on sooner than the high-olive blends, so don't over-blend. Stop at light trace and go straight to the pour — there are 12 cavities to fill

### Step 6 — Fragrance
None. The only scent in this bar is the cocoa butter.

### Step 7 — Pour, Spritz, Rest *(24–48h in mold)*
1. Pour evenly into the 12 cavities, alternating between the two molds so neither is filled from the thickened bottom of the pot; tap each mold to release bubbles; smooth the tops
2. Spritz the tops with 99% isopropyl alcohol to prevent soda ash
3. Bridge cardboard over both molds on risers so nothing touches the soap — individual cavities cool fast, and the cover keeps gel even
4. Leave undisturbed **24–48 hours** (a few days is fine)

### Step 8 — Unmold
1. Wait until the bars are firm and not sticky. Give them more time if they're soft
2. Flex the silicone and pop each bar out
3. No cutting — bars are individually molded. **The cure clock starts at the unmold**, so write the date down

### Step 9 — Cure *(6–8 weeks)*
1. Rack the bars with airflow on all sides; cool, dry, out of direct sun
2. **4 weeks minimum; 6–8 is better.** A 7% superfat bar keeps getting milder and harder with time, and this batch is in no hurry
3. Label with the batch number and the unmold date
