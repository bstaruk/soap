# Shea & Cocoa Butter Bar — Unscented

### Brambleberry 6-Cavity Oval — Cold Process
### Batch #7 — Ready

> **Mold:** Brambleberry 6-Cavity Oval (3.5" × 2.5" × 1" per cavity)
> **Yield:** 6 bars | **Cure:** 6–8 weeks (4 minimum) | **Active:** ~1 hour
> **Basis:** a new line — the first batch with shea and cocoa butter, and the first with no fragrance. A bath bar that also works at the sink
> **Safety:** glasses and gloves on before touching lye · lye into water, never the reverse · ventilate · soap-only equipment

## Recipe

| Component | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Olive oil | 241g | 45% — extra virgin |
| Coconut oil, refined 76° | 107g | 20% |
| Shea butter | 80g | 15% — first batch with shea; refined or unrefined, same lye |
| Cocoa butter | 54g | 10% — first batch with cocoa butter; **unrefined (not deodorized)**, because it is the bar's only scent |
| Castor oil | 54g | 10% — lather |
| Distilled water | 204g | 38% of oils |
| Sodium hydroxide (lye) | 71g | SoapCalc-confirmed (71.49); 45/20/15/10/10 @ 7% SF, 536g oils |
| Sodium lactate | 6ml | Into the cooled lye solution |
| Kaolin clay | 11g | Stick-blend into the oils before the lye |
| Fragrance | none | Unscented by design |

> **7% superfat** — a point above the hand bar, for the mildest bar in the archive; the hard butters offset the softness a higher superfat usually costs. Blend **45% olive / 20% coconut / 15% shea / 10% cocoa butter / 10% castor**: olive drops below the 62% house floor to make room for 25% butters, coconut stays inside its 18–28% rail, and castor stays pinned at 10%. No fragrance, so nothing browns and nothing accelerates. Fills the oval to 94.1% — 6 bars.
>
> **SoapCalc entries for the cross-check:** Olive Oil 45% · Coconut Oil, 76 deg 20% · Shea Butter 15% (not "Shea Oil, fractionated") · Cocoa Butter 10% · Castor Oil 10% — 536g oils, 7% superfat, water as 38% of oils. The calculator gives 71.49g before rounding.

## Instructions

### Step 1 — Gear Up
Glasses and gloves on. Ventilation on. Pre-weigh every ingredient into its own container before anything is combined. There is no fragrance to stage.

### Step 2 — Prep the Mold
Set the oval mold on a flat surface. No prep.

### Step 3 — Lye Solution *(make it first — it needs ~30 min to cool)*
1. Weigh **204g distilled water** into the 2qt stainless bowl
2. Weigh **71g lye** separately
3. **Pour lye into water** — never the reverse. Stir until clear; it heats to ~185°F and gives off brief fumes. Don't lean over it
4. Set it aside to cool to **110–120°F**
5. Stir **6ml sodium lactate** into the cooled solution

### Step 4 — Butters, Oils, Clay
1. Melt **54g cocoa butter**, **80g shea butter** and **107g coconut oil** together in the 6qt stockpot on the induction element, on low, until completely clear — no specks left
2. Take the pot off the heat and add **241g olive** and **54g castor**
3. **Stick-blend 11g kaolin clay straight into the combined oils** until smooth — no slurry cup
4. Bring the oils to **110–120°F**, within ~10°F of the lye solution. Stay in the upper half of the usual window: cocoa butter starts to set below ~95°F, and a cooling pot thickens into a false trace

### Step 5 — Combine and Trace
1. Pour the cooled lye solution into the stockpot — blend in place, no transfer
2. Stick-blend in **5–10 second bursts**, alternating with hand stirring, until **light trace** (the batter thickens just enough to leave a faint trail off the blender)
3. The butters bring trace on sooner than the high-olive blends, so don't over-blend. Stop at light trace and go straight to the pour

### Step 6 — Fragrance
None. The only scent in this bar is the cocoa butter.

### Step 7 — Pour, Spritz, Rest *(24–48h in mold)*
1. Pour evenly into the 6 cavities; tap the mold to release bubbles; smooth the tops
2. Spritz the tops with 99% isopropyl alcohol to prevent soda ash
3. Bridge cardboard over the mold on risers so nothing touches the soap — individual cavities cool fast, and the cover keeps gel even
4. Leave undisturbed **24–48 hours** (a few days is fine)

### Step 8 — Unmold
1. Wait until the bars are firm and not sticky. Give them more time if they're soft
2. Flex the silicone and pop each bar out
3. No cutting — bars are individually molded. **The cure clock starts at the unmold**, so write the date down

### Step 9 — Cure *(6–8 weeks)*
1. Rack the bars with airflow on all sides; cool, dry, out of direct sun
2. **4 weeks minimum; 6–8 is better.** A 7% superfat bar keeps getting milder and harder with time, and this batch is in no hurry
3. Label with the batch number and the unmold date
